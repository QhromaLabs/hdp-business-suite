# Business Suite System Overview

The HDP Business Suite provides an end-to-end Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and operational management system designed for multi-branch operations, logistics, sales tracking, and financial oversight.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Modules

### 1. Financial & Accounting Management
- **General Ledger & Chart of Accounts:** Complete multi-level account code tracking.
- **Journal Entries & Reconciliations:** Double-entry journal vouchers with audit verification.
- **Trial Balance & Profit/Loss Reporting:** Automated financial reporting across accounting periods.

### 2. Commercial & Sales Operations
- **Order Management & Quotations:** Sales order processing with customer ledger mapping.
- **Invoicing & POS Integration:** Real-time billing and sales receipt settlement.
- **Customer CRM & Receivables:** Aging reporting and credit management.

### 3. Supply Chain & Inventory Control
- **Multi-Warehouse Stock Tracking:** Real-time quantity balances, stock movements, and transfer vouchers.
- **Procurement & Purchase Orders:** Vendor management, receipt notes (GRN), and accounts payable tracking.

### 4. Field Mobility (Android Apps)
- **Deliveries Mobile App:** Real-time dispatch management, driver routing, trip status updates, and proof-of-delivery capture.
- **Sales Mobile App:** On-the-go order creation, stock availability checks, and customer interaction logging for field agents.

---

## 🔒 Security & Access Architecture
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict operational permissions per department and branch.
- **Data Privacy & Integrity:** Enterprise-grade authentication and transport security for multi-branch operations.
